Meet Your Technology Partner

At Ayende CX, we built our business after spending years consulting with small and mid-sized businesses across different industries. Many shared the same challenges: scattered customer data, manual processes, limited visibility into sales activity, and no clear connection between their Point of Sales systems and how they engaged customers. We recognized a gap and decided to provide a service that brings clarity, structure, and measurable value to everyday business operations.

Who We Are

We help organizations move from guesswork to informed decision-making. As certified Solution Architects and experienced Software Engineers, we focus on practical solutions that strengthen customer relationships and improve how businesses operate. Our approach ensures that each client receives a tailored system built around real needs, real data, and real growth opportunities.

Our work centres on delivering CRM solutions that are accessible and scalable. We design and implement systems powered by integrated Point of Sales data, giving business owners a clear view of customer behaviour, purchase patterns, and opportunities for deeper engagement. Whether you need a Salesforce implementation, a custom CRM platform, or enhancements to your existing environment, we provide the expertise to guide every step.

Ayende CX is committed to long-term partnerships. With experience in software architecture, operational improvement, and customer management, we support businesses as they streamline processes, strengthen decision-making, and build sustainable growth. Our goal is simple: help local businesses use technology with confidence and achieve success through a data-driven customer experience.
