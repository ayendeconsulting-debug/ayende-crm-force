Executive Summary

AyendeCX is building the first multi-tenant POS-CRM platform designed specifically for African businesses, addressing a $4.2B market opportunity in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and South Africa.

The Problem

  • Fragmented Customer Data: Businesses lack adequate data on their customers, leading to lost opportunities for repeat business, personalized engagement, and revenue growth. Most African SMBs have no unified view of customer purchase history or preferences.
  • Currency Volatility: Existing POS systems lock businesses into USD/EUR pricing, exposing them to exchange rate fluctuations that can erode margins overnight.
  • Unreliable Connectivity: Over 60% of African retail locations experience intermittent internet, making cloud-only solutions impractical for daily operations.
  • One-Size-Fits-All Solutions: Global POS platforms don't account for hybrid retail/rental models, mobile money integration, or informal supplier relationships common in African markets.

The Solution

An offline-first, multi-currency POS-CRM platform that unifies customer data, works without internet, and is specifically priced for the African SMB market ($50-200/mo).

Key Features

  • Offline-first architecture with automatic sync when connected
  • Multi-currency support (NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR, USD)
  • Integrated CRM with customer purchase history and loyalty programs
  • Targeted messaging and re-engagement campaigns
  • Multi-tenant SaaS model for scalability
